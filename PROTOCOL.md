# Establishment growth signals: fixed retrospective design

## Decision, audience and acceptance

Can a simple cross-state pool improve a prior-year baseline for published job growth at continuing establishments, while keeping establishment entry separate? The deliverable is a complete finite model/denominator comparison for public 2023-release BDS state-sector aggregates. It supports evidence-based interpretation of local industry signals. It does not measure worker hires, vacancies, labor shortages, causal business effects or employment opportunity for an individual.

Success requires a specific design before an analyzable snapshot or analytical outcomes are inspected; source/header/rights/version/classification/suppression checks; independent projection and SQL controls; all five validation candidates with a frozen global choice before held summaries; independent past-only donor/metric reconstruction, retained failures and exact/tolerance-declared isolated replay; executive-readable findings, original checked figures and a fully rendered independent manuscript; bounded privacy/canonical Git and exact-head Linux/merge/tag proof. Required unknown or failed gates block release. Negative or baseline-winning results stay negative. All50 studies are not complete until each has actual release evidence.

## Source, unit and disclosure protection

Use the official 2023-release BDS State by Sector CSV linked from the Census dataset page, 2010–2023, fifty states plus DC and the nineteen 2017-NAICS sectors listed in protocol.json. Exact source header aliases/flag representation must be inspected and documented after this commit before projection. The current codebook explicitly identifies this release and classification vintage. The general methodology page carries an outdated-guidance notice; describe the captured 2023 publication and its disclosed qualifications rather than claiming its procedures represent all current/future releases.

Only requested public keys/counts/denominator and supplied flags are analyzed. Raw complete source files and manuals remain private. Preserve D (disclosure), S (quality), X (structural) and N (unavailable rate), unknown tokens and nulls distinctly. No arbitrary blank-to-zero conversion, interpolation, imputation, suppression recovery or reconstruction of a hidden component. A target is valid only when its directly published continuer-creation count is numeric/nonnegative and denom positive. Do not derive a missing continuer count by subtracting a published birth count from a total. Retain every exclusion and its exact reason. No private worker/employer microdata, addresses, medical data or job-search campaign records are involved.

Verify raw/member/projection hashes and before/after metadata, unique year/state/sector keys, complete token inventories and coverage. Independently parse every selected source scalar with standard CSV and pandas C-parser; independently reduce the projection with SQL. These checks establish snapshot consistency, not source truth or complete labor-market coverage. Published counts may be disclosure perturbed, rounded and revised. Do not denoise or force arithmetic closure. Total/birth/continuer and destruction decompositions are complete, explicitly qualified source diagnostics; do not suppress an inconvenient discrepancy or infer a confidential value.

## Fixed cohort and predictor

The target is 100 times the directly published annual job_creation_continuers divided by reported denom (DHS-average employment). It measures net employment gain at continuing establishments that expanded between March observations; it is not gross hires or vacancies. Use the supplied denominator rather than reconstructing it from successive published employment cells: ownership/classification can change. BLS job-flow definitions and established job-creation research inform interpretation; do not join incompatible BLS quarterly populations to the annual Census counts.

A baseline state-sector series must have all six 2010–2015 years with valid direct target and denom at least 1000 each year. Require at least 50 eligible series. No rate-based screen. Preserve baseline exclusions and actual eligible state/sector counts. For each target year t, use own valid t-1 rate and a same-sector pool of other baseline-cohort states with valid t-1 counts/denominators; require at least five donors. The pool is 100 times total donor continuer creation divided by total donor denom. The target state's previous count is excluded from the cross-state pool but retained for its own lag. Missing inputs/targets are explicit case exclusions, never silent zeros. Current-year target information is never an input.

Five untuned candidate own-history weights are 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1. Predict lambda times own prior-year rate plus one-minus-lambda times the cross-state prior. Lambda 1 is the simple own-lag baseline and remains a candidate. Validation targets are 2016–2019. Choose one global lambda by first averaging absolute errors within each state-sector series, then equally across series; exact score ties prefer smaller lambda. Retain all scores and case counts. No iterative optimizer, complex machine learning, Bayesian hierarchy or exhaustive hyperparameter claim.

Freeze source/protocol/model/checker, validation outputs and chosen weight before held targets 2020–2023. All source inputs/quality are visible without blinding. The 2023 release was published later and revised historical values are not original vintages. Complete prior-year inputs are an idealized retrospective assumption, not verified real-time availability. No passed prospective leakage gate or operational forecast readiness claim.

## Complete held evidence and interpretation

Report equal-series MAE/MSE, derived RMSE, denominator-weighted MAE, every held year and sector, all per-series paired selected-minus-baseline losses, coverage/exclusions and actual observations. Report the fixed selected model and baseline in all three prior-denom subsets (at least 1000, 5000, 25000). These untuned size sensitivities do not select a new model or prove a shortage threshold. Show all failures and retained composition diagnostics. No significance, confidence coverage, causal or realized-benefit claim is prespecified.

The independent checker may not import model/metric helpers. Reconstruct baseline cohorts, eligible target cases and leave-one-state-out donor sums with SQLite, then use independent rational arithmetic for every prediction and exported loss/selection. Define any display-only floating tolerance before calculation. Isolated replay must regenerate the complete candidate/held outputs and frozen selection; do not replace a Linux failure by relaxing a tolerance after outcomes. Presentation must distinguish measured published-cell errors, synthetic/idealized assumptions, practical next evidence and unknowns. Confirm figure values/units/labels and inspect all final manuscript pages.

This scopes the broader historical proposal to annual state-sector partial pooling and composition accounting. Quarterly timing, change-point detection, R execution and full hiring-demand estimates are not implemented or claimed. New experiments/extensions require a new prospective design. Independent reviewed program manuscripts may be public; formal-publication projects including Azure optimization remain private.

Before any validation/model outcomes, scoring precision is fixed at twelve decimal places in percentage-point units, with nearest/ties-to-even rounding. Compute each target and prediction from exact source rationals, then export that fixed decimal representation; exact losses refer to those exported decimals. This untuned representational choice bounds aggregate arithmetic across unrelated denominators. It can change a per-case absolute loss by at most one trillionth of a percentage point relative to the same unrounded values, and does not imply that source measurement is that precise. Derived RMSE alone uses a deterministic decimal square root rounded to twelve places for display.
